Where I teach

Undergraduate, postgraduate and school audiences (KS2 is ages 7 to 11, KS5 is sixth form).

  1. 2026 to present

    Fieldwork Skills

    UCL, Postgraduate Teaching Associate, undergraduate

    Helping undergraduates step out of the classroom and into the field: planning safely, asking good questions and learning to read a place for themselves.

  2. 2026 to present

    Integrating Science in Disaster Risk Reduction

    UCL, Postgraduate Teaching Associate, postgraduate

    Supporting postgraduates to bridge the gap between scientific evidence and real-world risk decisions, so research actually reaches the people it is meant to protect.

  3. 2025 to present

    Emergency Planning & Management

    UCL, Postgraduate Teaching Associate, postgraduate

    Leading seminars on how we prepare for, respond to and recover from emergencies, bringing real cases to life and making space for every voice in the room.

  4. 2025 to present

    Climate Action & Disaster Resilience

    STEM Learning Scheme, Climate Ambassador, KS2

    Visiting primary schools to talk with children about climate change, nature and staying safe in emergencies, leaving them with hope rather than fear.

  5. 2025 to present

    Career in Science

    Brightside Mentoring, Mentor, KS5

    Mentoring sixth formers as they find their way into science, and sharing the honest, winding route that got me here.

  6. 2024 to present

    Co-learning for Disaster & Climate Justice

    UCL, Guest Lecturer, postgraduate

    Guest sessions on Green Learning for disaster resilience, using storytelling and creative activities to explore whose knowledge counts and how our relationship with nature shapes justice.

Teaching philosophy

I embed teaching within applied research rather than treating it as a separate role. My goal is to equip students with critical thinking and practical tools for navigating disaster risk.
