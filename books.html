Books

The books thatlit the way

The reading that shaped how I think about nature, power, disaster and the mind, and why each one stayed with me.

Nature and our place in it

Where my research begins: the idea that we belong to the living world, not above it.

Silent Spring

Silent Spring

Rachel Carson

Proof that good science can expose the truth, even when it means standing up to capitalism, and a reminder to keep going despite personal struggle.

The Biophilia Hypothesis

The Biophilia Hypothesis

Stephen Kellert & Edward O. Wilson (eds.)

Our belonging to nature, put into words.

The Posthuman

The Posthuman

Rosi Braidotti

Reimagining our role in this world, seeing beyond just the physical, and always striving to be and do better.

Miyazakiworld

Miyazakiworld

Susan Napier

Stepping into a simpler world with deep hidden meanings about our present, and allowing nature to quell the fires within our hearts.

Dune

Dune

Frank Herbert

Imagining what our present failure to support and appreciate nature may result in, far in the future.

Power, politics and injustice

Why systems fail people, and what it takes to change them.

Death Note

Death Note

Tsugumi Ohba & Takeshi Obata

A reflection on the injustices of the world, and how inaction and complacency result in the eventual destruction of humanity. It went on to inspire my paper, Already an Injustice.

Žižek

Living in the End Times and Too Late to Awaken

Slavoj Žižek

Two books on the overall failure of capitalism as a system of governance.

Honourable Friends?

Honourable Friends?

Caroline Lucas

The failings of the British government, and how to navigate politics to make change.

Into the dark

Disaster, the mind, and the truths we would rather not look at.

When the Dust Settles

When the Dust Settles

Lucy Easthope

The realities of post-disaster work.

The Skeleton Cupboard

The Skeleton Cupboard

Tanya Byron

The struggles of the mental health system, and the darkness we hide from daily reality.

House of the Sleeping Beauties

House of the Sleeping Beauties

Yasunari Kawabata

A reminder that behind the stable and ignorant realities we create, we may be casting aside real truths that are dark and growing. Without our attention, they will only get worse and bigger.

Mind, belief and discipline

What I believe, what I don't, and what I'm still open to.

The God Delusion

The God Delusion

Richard Dawkins

Detaching from religion's simple answers to complex questions. Science and personal philosophy became my anchors, along with an acceptance of the unknown, without the need to attribute it to a specific divinity.

Many Lives, Many Masters

Many Lives, Many Masters

Brian Weiss

Perhaps there is more to the mental and physical realms, beyond the current scope of scientific understanding.

The Marshmallow Test

The Marshmallow Test

Walter Mischel

Self-control is trained and practised. We can all do it, and as adults we are accountable to ourselves.
